# KramOS visual prompts

## Shared visual language

- Premium isometric 3D compliance and document-control diorama.
- Electric cobalt-blue machines and secure cabinets.
- Polished gold connectors, evidence chains and controlled gates.
- Clean white paper, translucent glass and precise cyan scanning light.
- Green for verified, amber for human review, restrained coral-red for missing evidence.
- Pure black `#000000` background, no floor or environmental scene.
- No readable text, letters, logos, signatures, watermarks or invented legal seals.
- Every connection must have an operational meaning.

### Alpha-safe generation notes

- Background must be exactly `#000000`, with no gradient, floor shadow or vignette.
- Keep dark-blue objects separated from black using thin cyan or gold rim light.
- Maintain at least `10%` empty space around the complete diorama.
- Generate without text; keep titles and explanations as real HTML on the website.
- Remove black using the existing soft-matte workflow, then export transparent PNG/WebP.

## Flux starting settings

- Outcome and dashboard icons: `1024×1024`.
- KramOS workflow and portfolio workflow: `1536×864` or `1344×768`.
- Steps: `30–40`.
- Guidance: `4.5–6`.
- Generate each outcome separately.
- Prefer medium detail over tiny decorative elements that will disappear on mobile.

## Quick validation checklist

- Each outcome remains understandable without its page title.
- Findings visibly connect to document, page, rule and human action.
- Human approval cannot be bypassed in the composition.
- No generated legal language, seals, logos or invented data appear.
- All objects remain inside the frame and detach cleanly from pure black.
- Video preserves interface text and starts and ends on stable frames.
