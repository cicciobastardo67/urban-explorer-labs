# Urban Explorer Labs — Product Pages and Marketing Plan

*Prepared: 2026-07-22*

## Positioning decision

**Category:** Cambodia-built private automation systems for sales, media, and compliance workflows.

**Homepage promise:** Private automation, built around Cambodian business.

**Supporting message:** Sales, media, and compliance systems designed for local workflows, multilingual teams, human approval, and data customers keep under their control.

Do not position Urban Explorer Labs as a generic software agency, chatbot vendor, social scheduler, or generic technology company. Public copy must avoid “AI” and “artificial intelligence”; use intelligent automation, sales automation, document intelligence, media automation, and private automation infrastructure.

## Page brief — LUYAGENT

**SEO title:** LUYAGENT | Private Sales Automation for Cambodian Sellers

**Hero:** Turn customer chats into confirmed orders—without losing control.

**Subhead:** LUYAGENT answers product questions, guides customers through ordering, tracks stock and payment states, and hands sensitive conversations to staff. Seller data stays with the business.

**Workflow proof:** Customer asks → product/FAQ context → order details → stock check → KHQR/manual payment confirmation → seller confirmation → receipt/handoff.

**Capabilities to prove:** channel support, Khmer/English conversation, catalog onboarding, stock/order states, payment confirmation boundary, human handoff, seller dashboard, receipts, corrections.

**Pilot:** 30-day Sales Rescue Pilot—one channel, one store, up to 100 products, KHQR flow, and human handoff.

**Pilot metrics:** median response time, completed orders, abandoned conversations, handoff rate, and staff time saved.

**Main alternatives:** manual staff + Meta Business Suite, Sousdey, TobSar, OrraChat, global chatbot/CRM tools, custom agencies.

**Defensible message:** Never lose a chat order. Keep customer, payment, stock, and order records on infrastructure you control.

## Page brief — KHAudit

**SEO title:** KHAudit | Evidence-Linked Compliance & Document Intelligence

**Hero:** Turn document piles into evidence-linked review workflows.

**Subhead:** KHAudit reads PDF, DOCX, and scanned documents in Khmer, English, and Chinese; extracts key fields; applies deterministic checks; and gives reviewers cited findings and clear next actions.

**Workflow proof:** ingest → OCR/layout extraction → structured fields → rule checks → cited finding/page/confidence → reviewer correction/approval → export package and audit trail.

**Guardrail:** Never promise automatic compliance, legal advice, or a guaranteed audit pass.

**Pilot:** Audit Readiness Sprint—one factory, one verified standard, a controlled document sample, evidence-gap register, and corrective-action workflow.

**Pilot metrics:** processing time, citation accuracy, missing records identified, review time, and gaps closed before audit.

**Main alternatives:** shared drives/Excel/manual review, consultants, Better Work services, QIMAone, Worldly, Angkor Compliance, standalone OCR tools.

**Defensible message:** Turn a multilingual document pile into an evidence-linked audit package—without replacing the auditor or inventing findings.

## Page brief — KhmerADV

**SEO title:** KhmerADV | Local-First Media Automation for Cambodian Teams

**Hero:** Move from campaign brief to approved content in one controlled workflow.

**Subhead:** KhmerADV connects copy, image production, brand adaptation, review, and multi-channel publishing while keeping human approval before anything goes live.

**Workflow proof:** structured brief → channel copy → ComfyUI imagery → branded output → Telegram review/approve/reject → publishing queue.

**Pilot:** Campaign Factory Pilot—one campaign, 12 approved assets, two confirmed channels, and one approval workflow.

**Pilot metrics:** time per approved asset, revisions, production cost, approval turnaround, and publishing lead time.

**Main alternatives:** Meta Business Suite, Canva, freelancers/agencies, Buffer, Hootsuite, disconnected generation tools.

**Defensible message:** Turn one approved brief into a controlled set of branded assets while keeping working files under customer control.

## Evidence and claim ledger

Every public claim must be tagged internally as one of:

- **Verified:** implemented and demonstrated now
- **Needs confirmation:** likely true, owner must confirm exact boundary
- **Roadmap:** planned, not marketed as available
- **Do not publish:** unverified outcome, guarantee, or risky assertion

Before page copy is finalized, lock these facts:

1. Customer-facing names: LUYAGENT versus AgentKhmer; KHAudit versus Agent Khmer OS; KhmerADV versus Hermes Media.
2. Production-ready channels and integrations for each product.
3. Exact local/private boundary, including any optional external service.
4. Pricing stance: quote-only, setup + subscription, hardware + subscription, or paid pilot.
5. Approved screenshots, demos, metrics, customers, and testimonials.
6. Real contact route and form delivery endpoint.
7. Deployment, onboarding, support area, warranty, backups, and retention promises.

## Content strategy

### Pillar 1 — Cambodian sales operations

- How Cambodian social sellers can reduce missed chat orders
- KHQR order confirmation workflow: what should remain human-approved
- Facebook/Telegram order tracking without spreadsheet chaos
- Comparison content only after product readiness and claim review

### Pillar 2 — Audit readiness and evidence

- Multilingual document preparation for Cambodian factory audits
- Evidence-linked findings versus generic document summaries

### Pillar 3 — Controlled media production

- Local-first brand asset workflows
- Human approval before automated publishing

Each pillar starts with one decision-stage product page and one searchable guide. Case studies follow only after measurable pilots.

## 90-day go-to-market sequence

### Days 1–15 — Facts and proof

- Complete 10 factory, 10 seller, and 8 marketing-team interviews.
- Confirm names, readiness, privacy boundaries, pricing hypothesis, and CTA route.
- Record one two-minute product workflow per product.
- Create battlecards: Sousdey/TobSar; Angkor Compliance/QIMAone; Hootsuite/Buffer.

### Days 16–45 — Pages and pilots

- Build KHAudit first, then LUYAGENT, then KhmerADV.
- Launch one paid design-partner pilot per product.
- Implement real form delivery and analytics events.
- Add first-party screenshots and explicit guardrails.

### Days 46–90 — Evidence and distribution

- Publish one case study per successful pilot.
- Publish one searchable guide per product pillar.
- Founder-led outreach through relevant Cambodian business and industry networks.
- Measure qualified assessments, pilot conversion, page CTA rate, and sales-cycle objections.
