# Hermes Post visual prompts

## Shared visual language

- Premium isometric 3D operational diorama.
- Electric cobalt blue, clean cyan light, polished gold edges and rails.
- Restrained green for approved or scheduled states; warm amber for review.
- Friendly, simple small-business tools rather than complex agency machinery.
- Pure black `#000000` background for easy removal and transparent export.
- No readable text, letters, logos, watermarks or recognizable platform branding.
- One clear operational idea per asset, with generous empty borders.

### Shared negative prompt

```text
letters, words, captions, readable text, typography, watermark, signature, brand logo, social media logo, random symbols, distorted screen, deformed objects, duplicate objects, floating disconnected parts, clutter, excessive neon, purple-dominant palette, cartoon, flat vector art, low-poly, blurry, grain, chromatic aberration, harsh bloom, cropped object, cut-off edges, grey background, gradient background
```

## Flux starting settings

- Icons: `1024×1024`.
- Workflow explainer: `1536×864` or `1344×768`.
- Steps: `28–36`.
- Guidance: `4.5–6`.
- Generate each icon separately.
- Remove the pure-black background only after selecting the best result.
- Export final website icons as transparent PNG or WebP.

## Quick validation checklist

- The concept remains understandable when shown without the page copy.
- No generated words or logos appear inside the artwork.
- The full object stays inside the frame with at least 8–12% empty margin.
- Outcome icons share camera angle, palette, material and lighting.
- Video starts and ends on stable frames and does not distort interface text.
